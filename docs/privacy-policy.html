Privacy Policy · Gizlilik Politikası
100% On-Device · Zero Data Collection

1. Overview

SpectraS (package com.alisamilaytekin.spectras) is an offline infrared & pro color-grading RAW camera and photo editor application. Your privacy is fundamental: SpectraS processes every camera frame, RAW (DNG) capture, and edited photograph strictly on your device.

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal data.

  • SpectraS does not require account registration or sign-in.
  • SpectraS does not request internet permission and does not upload photos, EXIF metadata, analytics, or crash logs to any server.
  • Custom color presets and app preferences are stored solely in your device's local private storage (SharedPreferences).

3. Device Permissions

  • Camera (CAMERA): Used exclusively to display the live camera preview and capture JPEG and RAW (DNG) photographs when you press the shutter button.
  • Storage on Android 5–9 (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used only on legacy Android versions (API 28 and below) to save captured and edited photos into your DCIM/SpectraS folder. On Android 10 and newer, SpectraS uses Android Scoped Storage (MediaStore) and the system Photo Picker without broad storage access.

4. Third-Party Services

The current version of SpectraS contains no third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or remote analytics libraries. If optional services (such as Google Play Billing or Google AdMob) are introduced in a future update, this Privacy Policy will be updated transparently beforehand.

5. Children's Privacy & Contact

Because SpectraS collects no personal information from anyone, it is safe for users of all ages. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at:

alisamilaytekin@gmail.com

Yürürlük Tarihi: 24 Kasım 2026
